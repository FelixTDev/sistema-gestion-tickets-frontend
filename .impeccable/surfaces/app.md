# App surface

Mode: Operate. Scope: public site, client portal, and staff portal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One sober service system makes orientation and ticket progress immediately legible; it refuses a generic banking dashboard assembled from interchangeable cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy fields, turquoise actions, green institutional accents, cool-gray surfaces, precise linear icons, 10–16 px radii, restrained layered shadows, Manrope display type, and Inter body type.

STORY: People identify the right path, understand the channel boundary, complete the permitted task, and read the resulting state without guesswork.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact institutional header anchors the top; the active task and its primary action dominate the left/center, while contextual status or guidance supports it without competing. On mobile, the task stacks before supporting context and the action remains reachable.

FORM: Pinned Figma Make application system, position 1 of 1; seed key: not applicable—brief-pinned authority.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
